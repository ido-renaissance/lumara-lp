Questions

Common Questions

What exactly do you do?+
We buy institutional allocations in late-stage private companies and split them into $25,000 positions for accredited investors. 25 years, $140M under management, 1,200 investors.
How does this work?+
Fifteen-minute call, accreditation verified, the memo walked through. E-sign, wire, and you hold a piece of the block. Exit at listing or acquisition.
Why has nobody offered me this before?+
Because rounds are sold in blocks of a million and up, and nothing in that process accepts $25,000. It is plumbing, not exclusivity. We built the missing piece.
What if the door is real but the deals are leftovers?+
Our own capital is in every position first, at the same terms you get. A firm does not put its own money into leftovers for 25 years and end up with 92% of its names public.
How do I get my money out?+
You wait. There is no exit until the company lists or is acquired, and these positions do not trade in between. This should never be money you need in the next four years.
When do these companies list?+
The names we buy are typically positioned to list within 18 to 36 months of our entry. That is a window based on where the company sits, not a promise. No listing is guaranteed.
Am I eligible?+
If you’re accredited, yes. Because this is a public offer, verification has to be real: statements, a CPA or attorney letter, or a third-party service. Most investors clear it in a day.
What does it cost?+
The minimum is $25,000 per position. The full fee structure is in the subscription documents and explained in writing on the call, before you commit.
Which companies are open right now?+
The list changes as blocks fill, so it lives on the call. Recent allocations have included names like OpenAI, Anthropic, Stripe and Databricks.
