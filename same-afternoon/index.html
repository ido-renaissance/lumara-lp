Questions

Common Questions

What exactly do you do?+
Lumara buys institutional allocations in late-stage private companies and splits them into $25,000 positions for accredited investors. 25 years, $140M under management, 1,200 investors.
How does this work?+
A fifteen-minute call verifies accreditation and walks the memo on current open names. E-sign, wire, hold. The position exits at listing or acquisition.
Isn’t “uncorrelated” just another word for “you can’t see the losses”?+
Partly, and we would rather say it than have you discover it. Private positions can lose value, and you will not see a daily mark. What you gain is exposure priced by company events instead of market afternoons. That trade is the product; make it with open eyes.
How do I get my money out?+
You wait for the listing or an acquisition. There is no daily liquidity, no early redemption. A private sleeve should never hold money you need in the next four years.
What are realistic expectations?+
We do not quote projected returns. 92% of the companies we have ever invested in have gone public, and positions typically sit 18 to 36 months from our entry to a listing window. No listing is guaranteed.
My advisor has never mentioned this. Why?+
Advisors sell what their platform carries, and late-stage private rounds are not on the shelf, because they are sold in seven-figure blocks. The Allocation Split is what puts a $25,000 door on that market.
How much of a portfolio does this usually touch?+
That is between you and your advisor. Positions start at $25,000 each, and most of our investors hold 3 to 5 names. We size allocations; we do not give financial advice.
Am I eligible?+
If you’re accredited, yes. Verification is real, as the law requires for a public offer: statements, a CPA or attorney letter, or a third-party service. Most clear it in a day.
What does it cost?+
The minimum is $25,000 per position. The full fee structure is in the subscription documents and walked through in writing on the call, before you commit to anything.
