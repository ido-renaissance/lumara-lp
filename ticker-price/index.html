Questions

Common Questions

What exactly do you do?+
Lumara is a private-markets fund — 25 years, $140M under management, 1,200 investors. We buy institutional allocations in late-stage private companies and split them into $25,000 positions for accredited investors, at the same price and terms as our own capital.
How does this work?+
A fifteen-minute call verifies your accreditation and walks the memo on what we currently have room in. E-sign, wire, and you hold a piece of the block we bought. The position exits at listing or acquisition.
Isn’t this just chasing the last cycle’s winners?+
The regret is the proof the asset class works, not the pitch. We are not selling you Facebook in 2026; we are selling the same seat, in this cycle’s names, before their tickers exist. What is open right now lives on the call.
How do I know I’m not too late on these names too?+
They are still private. That is the entire point — the private price still exists for every name on the current memo. Once a ticker appears, we are no longer buyers and neither are you, through us.
What are realistic expectations?+
We do not quote projected returns. 92% of the companies we have ever invested in have gone public, and positions typically sit 18 to 36 months from our entry to a listing window. No listing is guaranteed — a position can lose value or fail entirely.
How do I get my money out?+
You wait for the listing or an acquisition. There is no market for these positions in between, no early redemption. Do not put money here that you need in the next four years — that patience is the price of the private price.
Am I eligible?+
If you’re accredited, yes. Because this offer is made publicly, the law requires real verification — statements, a CPA or attorney letter, or a third-party service — not a checkbox. Most investors clear it in a day.
What does it cost?+
The minimum position is $25,000. The full fee structure is in the subscription documents and walked through in writing on the call, before you commit to anything.
Which companies do you have room in right now?+
The list changes as blocks fill, so it lives on the call. Recent allocations have included names like OpenAI, Anthropic, Stripe and Databricks.
