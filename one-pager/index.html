What’s On the Page

Lumara buys institutional allocations in late-stage private companies — names like OpenAI, Anthropic, Stripe and Databricks — and splits them into $25,000 positions on the same terms as its own capital. Each open name comes with a plain-English analyst memo: three pages, no deck, no webinar. Because the memo names live allocations, it is walked through with verified accredited investors on a fifteen-minute call. That call is the whole ask.

I

What the company does, in plain English - no adjectives doing the work numbers should do.

II

The round we entered: when, at what valuation, and why the desk took it after 25 years of passing on most things.

III

The terms you would hold - identical to the terms on Lumara’s own capital, from $25,000.

IV

The window: why the name is typically positioned to list within 18 to 36 months, and what has to stay true for that to hold.

V

The honest paragraph: the position is illiquid until a listing or acquisition, no listing is guaranteed, and this should never be money you need in the next four years.

Questions

Common Questions

Why can’t you just email me the list?+
Because it names live private allocations, and an offer like this is only open to verified accredited investors under Rule 506(c). Anyone who will email you that list on request is telling you what their diligence is worth. The call exists to verify you, then the memo is walked through name by name.
What exactly is Lumara?+
A private-markets fund with 25 years of history, $140M under management and 1,200 investors. We buy institutional blocks in late-stage private companies with our own capital in first, then split those positions into $25,000 pieces for our investors. We call it the Allocation Split.
What happens on the call?+
Fifteen minutes. Accreditation verified properly — statements, a CPA or attorney letter, or a third-party service, not a checkbox. Then the memo on each open name. You leave with the full picture and no obligation.
What are realistic expectations?+
We do not quote projected returns. The record: 92% of the companies we have ever invested in have gone public, counting every position the fund has ever taken. Positions typically sit 18 to 36 months from our entry to a listing window. Nothing is guaranteed.
How do I get my money out?+
At the listing or an acquisition, not before. These positions do not trade in between.
What does it cost?+
The minimum is $25,000 per position. The full fee structure is in the subscription documents and covered, in writing, on the call before you commit to anything.
